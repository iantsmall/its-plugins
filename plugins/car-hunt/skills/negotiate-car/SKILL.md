# Negotiate the car

Anchor the offer on facts the seller can't argue with: comparable listings, and documented defects with repair costs. Decide the walk-away number before any talking starts, because it's much harder to set once you want the car.

## Have these first

The fair range and walk-away number from `price-it-out`, the inspection findings with their costs, and two or three comparable listings. If any are missing, get them first, or say what the offer is missing.

## Build the offer

- **Opening offer:** the middle of the fair range, minus the documented repair costs, rounded. Never above the walk-away number.
- **The reason:** one or two sentences of facts, such as "The inspection found worn front brakes and a leaking valve-cover gasket, about $450 in parts and labor."
- **Room to move:** leave space for one or two small concessions.

## Private sellers

- Friendly and specific works best. Being ready to close quickly, with cash, is leverage.
- Meet at the seller's bank, or at a police department's safe-exchange spot. Pay by cashier's check or a transfer made at their bank.
- Get the title signed over, and a bill of sale with the VIN, price, date, both names and both signatures.
- Never wire money, and never pay a deposit by gift card, crypto or payment app to someone you haven't met.

## Dealers

- Negotiate the out-the-door price in writing, never the monthly payment.
- Ask for the fees itemized, and decline add-ons you didn't ask for.
- The FTC Buyers Guide ("As Is" or the warranty) is part of the deal. Get any promised repair written into the contract or the Guide.
- If borrowing, arrange your own loan approval first.

## Messages

Draft one to three short, polite messages: the opening offer, a reply to a counter-offer, and a walk-away note that leaves the door open.

## When to walk

Walk when the seller won't allow an inspection, the title or VIN doesn't check out, the price stays above the walk-away number, or there's pressure to decide on the spot. Say it once, then move on to the next car on the shortlist.
