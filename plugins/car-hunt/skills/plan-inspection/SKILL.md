# Plan the inspection

A listing shows what the seller wants seen, and a history report shows only what got reported. An independent inspection shows the car's actual condition. For a car you can't see, it's the only real look you'll get.

## Pick the inspector

- **Near the buyer:** a trusted independent shop with a lift.
- **Far away:** a mobile inspector who goes to the car. Lemon Squad starts at $219 and Vehicle Inspectors at $249 (2026 prices; re-check). Reviews are mixed, so send a specific checklist. Or call a well-reviewed independent shop near the seller, and ask the seller to drop the car off there.
- **Dealer return window:** with CarMax or Carvana, book an inspection for the first day or two after delivery.

A seller who won't allow an independent inspection is a reason to walk away.

## Ask the seller for

A cold start (the engine not run that day), daylight, and time on a lift if the inspection is at a shop.

## Inspector checklist (send this)

- **Start and scan:** a cold-start video, and an OBD scan covering stored and pending codes and the readiness monitors. Monitors that aren't ready can mean codes were cleared recently.
- **Engine and fluids:** the level, color and smell of the oil, coolant, transmission fluid and brake fluid; leaks; belts and hoses; smoke on start-up.
- **Running gear:** brakes and rotors; tires, including wear pattern and date codes; suspension and steering play; CV boots; exhaust.
- **Comfort and electrics:** the A/C blows cold, and every window, lock, light and gauge works.
- **Test drive:** town and highway, hard braking, and tight turns both ways (to catch an AWD groan), noting any shudder or slipping.
- **Underneath, with photos:** rocker panels, rear wheel arches, subframes and their mounts, brake and fuel lines, rear suspension arms, floor pans and the spare-tire well.
- **Flood:** silt under the carpets and in the spare-tire well, rust on the seat rails, corrosion under the dash, and a musty smell.
- **Paperwork:** the VIN on the dash, the door jamb and the title all match, and the title is in the seller's name.
- **This model's weak spots:** copy them from the car's file, where `vet-listing` writes them. If there are none yet, look up known problems for that model and year first.

## Sort the results

- **Walk away:** structural rust, flood signs, frame damage, or a failing engine or transmission.
- **Price it in:** repairs with a cost attached. Hand them to `price-it-out` and `negotiate-car`.
- **Normal wear:** note it and move on.

Add an "Inspection" section to the car's file.
