# Plan the board year

Read `board/board-profile.md`. If it does not exist, run `setup-board` first.

## Step 1: Count the meetings

Compare the planned meetings with the bylaw minimum. If the board has met fewer times than the minimum in the past 12 months, say so plainly and note it for the next meeting. Do not give legal advice about what it means; suggest the board president check with the corporation's lawyer if it is a worry.

## Step 2: Pick dates

Use the rhythm in the profile. For each meeting give:

- **Meeting date and time** (a weekday that fits the profile)
- **Packet goes out by:** at least 7 days before, which is well ahead of the bylaw notice minimum
- **Bylaw notice deadline:** the latest legal send date, shown so nobody cuts it close
- **Must decide:** the decisions this meeting exists for

Check each date against holidays and the organization's busy season (for a cat rescue, kitten season). Offer a second option for any date that lands badly.

## Step 3: Put the fixed items in the right meetings

Place these items, adjusting to what the bylaws in the profile say:

| Item | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Election of directors whose terms end | The meeting the bylaws name (often January) |
| Officer elections | At the same meeting, if terms line up |
| Approve the year's budget | First meeting of the year, or the last meeting of the prior year |
| Approve the delegation line (what operators decide alone) | First meeting after `setup-board` |
| Conflict-of-interest review and signed disclosures | First meeting of the year |
| Form 990 review | At least the number of days before filing that the bylaws require. For calendar-year filers the return is normally due May 15; confirm with whoever files it, because extensions exist |
| Review of the policy list (adoption, intake, foster, donor privacy, retention) | One policy per meeting, so it never becomes a marathon |
| Big projects check-in | Every meeting, using the master task tracker |

## Step 4: Add the calendar

Write `board/year-plan.md` as a table: Meeting, Date, Packet out by, Notice deadline, Must decide.

Offer to add the meetings to Google Calendar if it is connected. Create events only after the person agrees, and invite directors only after the person confirms the email addresses. Put "Packet out" as its own event a week earlier so the Secretary or whoever prepares it gets a reminder.

## Step 5: Hand off

Offer `prep-board-meeting` for the next meeting on the list.
