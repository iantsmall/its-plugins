# Run a board meeting

Read `board/board-profile.md` and today's `board/meetings/<date>/agenda.md`. The person asking is usually the President (the chair). Give them a short script they can read from.

## Before the call (5 minutes)

- Confirm who is taking minutes (the Secretary, per the profile). Tell them to record motions, who moved, who seconded, the vote, and every action with an owner and date.
- Open the packet and the master task tracker.
- Count directors expected. Quorum is in the profile (a majority of directors in office, per the bylaws).

## Script

**Call to order.** "It is [time]. This is the [regular/special] meeting of the board of Full Hearts Cat Rescue, Inc. Notice was sent on [date]."

**Quorum.** Call each director's name and have them answer on video so everyone hears. "We have [n] of [total]. That is a quorum." If there is no quorum, no business may be done. Say so, thank everyone, and move the decisions to an email approval (see `follow-up-board`).

**Conflicts.** "Does anyone have a conflict of interest on today's items?" Record each one. The person with a conflict leaves the video room (or goes on mute and off camera) for that item and does not vote. Pay particular attention to any item the tracker marks "Needs check".

**Consent items.** "Is there any item on the consent list anyone wants to pull out for discussion?" If none: "I move to approve the minutes of [date] and accept the written reports as presented." Second, call the vote.

**Each decision.** Keep time.

1. The presenter reads the question and recommendation in 2 minutes or less. The packet already covers background.
2. Questions of fact, then discussion. Stop discussion at the time limit. If it is not settled, ask: "Do we need more information, or do we vote?"
3. Motion: "I move that Full Hearts [do X] by [date], with [name] responsible." Someone seconds.
4. Vote by name, one at a time, on video. Say the count out loud: "[n] in favor, [n] opposed, [n] abstaining. The motion [passes/fails]."
5. Say the action: "[Name] will [do X] by [date]." The Secretary writes it down as spoken.

**Ties.** If votes tie, the bylaws in the profile say who may break the tie. Prefer to postpone and gather more information rather than use that power.

**Parking lot.** If a topic is not on the agenda, write it down and say when it will come back. Do not discuss it now.

**Close.** Read back every action: owner and date. Ask each owner to say "yes" out loud. "Our next meeting is [date]. Is there any other business that cannot wait?" Then: "Is there a motion to adjourn?"

## Keep it short and useful

- Stay inside 45 minutes unless the profile says otherwise. If time runs out, finish the current decision, then move the rest to email or the next meeting.
- Do not read the packet aloud.
- Operators do not report what they did unless it is unusual. The board's time is for decisions.
- If someone starts discussing something that needs a confidential session, a motion is needed first, with the reason recorded.

## After

Offer `write-minutes`.
