# Write board minutes

Read `board/board-profile.md` and the meeting's `agenda.md`. Ask for the notes, a transcript, or the Secretary's scribbles. Never invent a vote, a name, or a number. If something is missing, write "[missing: ask Secretary]" and list the gaps at the end.

## Minutes template

```
Full Hearts Cat Rescue, Inc.
Minutes of the [regular/special] meeting of the Board of Directors
Date: [Month D, YYYY]   Time: [start]–[end]   Place: Video meeting
Notice sent: [date, method]

Directors present: [names]
Directors absent: [names, excused or unexcused]
Others present: [names, role]
Quorum: [yes/no, n of total]
Called to order by [name] at [time]. Minutes taken by [name].

Conflicts declared: [none, or name, item, left the room]

Consent items
- Minutes of [date]: approved. Moved [name], seconded [name]. Vote [n–n–n].
- Reports accepted: [list]

Decisions
1. [Topic]
   Discussion in one or two sentences.
   Motion: [exact wording]. Moved [name], seconded [name].
   Vote: [yes names], [no names], [abstain names]. Result: passed/failed.

Actions
| # | Action | Owner | Due | Tracker ID |

Parking lot: [topics deferred and when they return]
Next meeting: [date, time]
Adjourned at [time].

Submitted by: [Secretary]     Approved on: [date]
```

## Rules

1. **Decisions, not conversations.** Minutes say what was decided, who voted how, and who does what by when. Do not transcribe discussion. One or two sentences of context are enough.
2. **Every action has one owner and one due date.** If the notes show an action with no owner or date, flag it. Do not guess.
3. **Public by default.** The bylaws in the profile make minutes public once accepted unless the board voted to keep a part confidential. Leave out donor details, staff matters and private personal information. If a confidential session happened, record only that it happened, the motion to hold it, who attended, and the time. Do not summarize what was said.
4. **Money in minutes.** Record decisions and approved amounts, not account numbers or full balances copied from reports.
5. **Names that match.** Use the names and offices in the profile.
6. **File names carry the date.** Save as `Minutes YYYY-MM-DD` and make sure the title inside shows the same date. Old file names with mismatched dates make records hard to trust.

## After

- Draft is due to the Secretary for approval within 5 days of the meeting, so memory is fresh.
- Save to `board/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD/minutes.md`. If Drive is connected and the person agrees, also file the minutes in the board minutes folder named in the profile.
- Minutes are kept permanently, according to the retention rules in the bylaws.
- Approved minutes are placed on the next meeting's consent list, or approved by email if the board uses that route.

Offer `follow-up-board` to turn the actions into tracker items and send the recap.
