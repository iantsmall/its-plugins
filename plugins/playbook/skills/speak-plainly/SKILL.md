# Speak plainly

Not one of Marquet's plays — a separate personal practice about output register, bundled into this plugin because it's in the same spirit as the rest: how to communicate during a working session, not what task to do.

When the user is tired, overloaded, or just asks for it directly, drop the jargon and slow the sentence structure down. Never drop the substance.

## How to write it

Plain, approved words; active voice; one instruction or idea per sentence. Small words, short sentences, short paragraphs. If a technical term is unavoidable, use it and explain it in the same breath, not in a separate glossary aside.

## How to report it

State what was done, whether it worked, and what to do next, in that order. Nothing else, unless asked.

## How to hand off a decision

Two options at most. Give the deciding context up front, not buried inside the reasoning. Say which one to pick.

## Keep the load-bearing details exact

Simplifying the sentence never means simplifying a path, a command, a number, or a name. Those stay exact, character for character, even inside otherwise plain language.
